CORPORATE PASSWORD POLICY
Document ID: POL-SEC-001
Version: 3.2
Effective Date: January 1, 2024
Review Date: January 1, 2025

1. PURPOSE
This policy establishes requirements for creating, managing, and protecting passwords used to access company information systems and resources.

2. SCOPE
This policy applies to all employees, contractors, temporary staff, and third-party users who access company systems.

3. PASSWORD REQUIREMENTS
3.1 Minimum length: 12 characters
3.2 Must contain at least one character from each of the following:
    - Uppercase letters (A-Z)
    - Lowercase letters (a-z)
    - Numbers (0-9)
    - Special characters (!@#$%^&*()-_+=)
3.3 Must not contain the user's name, username, or employee ID
3.4 Must not be a dictionary word or common phrase
3.5 Must not match any of the last 12 previously used passwords

4. PASSWORD EXPIRATION
4.1 Standard accounts: Passwords expire every 90 days
4.2 Administrative accounts: Passwords expire every 60 days
4.3 Service accounts: Passwords expire every 180 days
4.4 Users will receive notification 14 days before expiration

5. ACCOUNT LOCKOUT
5.1 Accounts lock after 5 consecutive failed login attempts
5.2 Lockout duration: 30 minutes (automatic unlock)
5.3 Manual unlock available via IT Support or self-service portal

6. MULTI-FACTOR AUTHENTICATION (MFA)
6.1 MFA is mandatory for all user accounts
6.2 Approved MFA methods: Microsoft Authenticator app, phone call, SMS
6.3 At least 2 MFA methods must be registered per account

7. PASSWORD STORAGE AND SHARING
7.1 Passwords must never be written down or stored in plain text
7.2 Use the company-approved password manager (LastPass Enterprise) for storing credentials
7.3 Never share passwords with anyone, including IT staff
7.4 IT staff will never ask for your password

8. SELF-SERVICE PASSWORD RESET
8.1 Available at https://passwordreset.company.com
8.2 Requires pre-registered security questions or MFA verification
8.3 Available 24/7 without IT Support intervention

9. VIOLATIONS
Violations of this policy may result in disciplinary action, including termination of employment or contract.
