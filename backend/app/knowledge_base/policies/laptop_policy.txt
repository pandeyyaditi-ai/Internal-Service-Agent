CORPORATE LAPTOP AND HARDWARE POLICY
Document ID: POL-HW-001
Version: 2.0
Effective Date: January 1, 2024
Review Date: January 1, 2025

1. PURPOSE
This policy governs the issuance, use, maintenance, and return of company-provided laptops and hardware equipment.

2. SCOPE
All employees and contractors who are issued company hardware.

3. HARDWARE STANDARDS
3.1 Developer Configuration:
    - MacBook Pro 14" M3 Pro or Dell XPS 15
    - 16GB RAM minimum (32GB available upon request)
    - 512GB SSD
    - External monitor: Dell U2723QE 27" 4K

3.2 Business Configuration:
    - Dell Latitude 14" or HP EliteBook
    - 16GB RAM
    - 256GB SSD
    - External monitor: Dell P2422H 24" FHD

3.3 Standard Accessories:
    - Wireless keyboard and mouse
    - USB-C docking station
    - Headset (for remote workers)

4. HARDWARE REFRESH CYCLE
4.1 Laptops are refreshed every 3 years
4.2 Monitors and accessories: every 5 years
4.3 Eligible employees will be notified by IT

5. REQUESTING HARDWARE
5.1 Standard requests: https://itportal.company.com/hardware-request
5.2 Manager approval required
5.3 Timeline: 5-10 business days for standard equipment
5.4 Emergency replacements: same-day loaner available from IT Help Desk

6. EMPLOYEE RESPONSIBILITIES
6.1 Treat company hardware with reasonable care
6.2 Do not install unauthorized software
6.3 Enable full disk encryption (BitLocker/FileVault)
6.4 Keep operating system and software updated
6.5 Report loss, theft, or damage immediately to IT Security
6.6 Do not modify hardware (RAM upgrades, etc.) without IT approval

7. RETURN POLICY
7.1 All hardware must be returned upon separation from the company
7.2 Hardware must be returned to IT Help Desk on or before the last working day
7.3 Data will be securely wiped before reuse or disposal
7.4 Failure to return hardware may result in payroll deduction

8. LOSS AND THEFT
8.1 Report immediately to IT Security (security@company.com)
8.2 IT will remotely lock and wipe the device
8.3 File a police report for theft
8.4 Replacement device issued within 1-2 business days

9. PERSONAL USE
9.1 Limited personal use is permitted
9.2 Company reserves the right to monitor all activity on company devices
9.3 No expectation of privacy on company hardware
